StayInContact Privacy Policy

Applies to the “StayInContact” app for iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

1. At a glance

Everything you enter in StayInContact stays on your device only. I receive none of it.

  • no user account and no sign-in,
  • no cloud and no server of my own: the app does not send your entries anywhere,
  • no tracking, no analytics tools and no advertising,
  • no sharing or selling of data to third parties.

2. Controller

Saša Stjepanović, GenZ Dev (sole proprietorship)
Kraepelinstr. 63, 80804 Munich, Germany
Phone: +49 152 03907019
Email: support@genz-dev.com

No data protection officer has been appointed, as there is no legal obligation to do so.

3. Data stored only on your device

To work, the app stores the following information locally in the app's protected storage on your device:

  • people you add: name, phone number, profile picture, notes, place, category and desired contact rhythm,
  • when you got in touch with a person,
  • your own categories and your settings (e.g. your name, language, style, reminder time).

This data never leaves your device. You can change or delete it in the app at any time, one by one or via Settings → Reset all data. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Whether your device includes the app's storage in its own device backup (e.g. iCloud Backup or Android backup) is up to you in your device settings. Apple's or Google's privacy policies apply to such backups.

4. Permissions

The app only asks for permissions when you use the related feature. You can revoke them at any time in your device settings.

Contacts

When you import a person from your address book, the app reads only the selected contact (name and phone number) and saves it as an entry in StayInContact. The app does not change or upload your address book.

Photos

When you choose a profile picture, the app only receives the selected photo. It is stored locally in the app. The app does not use the camera.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications directly on your device. No server and no push service is used.

5. Calls, messages and WhatsApp

When you tap “Call”, “SMS” or “WhatsApp” in the app, StayInContact opens the corresponding app on your device with the person's phone number. Only then is data handed over to that app. WhatsApp's use of data is governed by the privacy policy of WhatsApp Ireland Ltd.

6. Backup

In the settings you can create a backup file of your entries. Where you save it (e.g. Files app, cloud storage or an email to yourself) is entirely up to you via your device's share menu. The app does not transfer the file on its own. When restoring, the app only reads the file you select.

7. Purchases and tips

Unlocks and tips are processed exclusively through Apple's App Store or Google Play. You only enter payment details with Apple or Google. I do not receive any payment or account data, only the information that a purchase took place and aggregated sales reports. The privacy policies of Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland) and Google (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland) apply.

So that purchased features stay unlocked after reinstalling, the app asks Apple or Google whether you have already bought them.

8. Ratings

After some use, the app may ask whether you would like to rate it. For this it uses Apple's or Google's rating dialog. Whether and how you rate goes directly to Apple or Google. I do not receive any data about you.

9. Crash reports and store statistics

Apple and Google provide developers with aggregated statistics, such as the number of downloads. I only receive crash reports if you have agreed to share them with developers in your device settings. These reports contain technical details about the crash, but no content from the app.

10. Support by email

If you write to me, e.g. via Settings → Feedback & Support, I process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete the message once it has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16. As the app does not transmit any data to me, I do not collect personal data from children either.

12. Your rights

You have the right at any time to:

  • access your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR).

The data in the app is stored only on your device, where you can view, change, export and delete it yourself. For anything else, a short message to support@genz-dev.com is enough.

13. Right to lodge a complaint

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for me is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

14. Changes

I update this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies. The privacy policy for the website genz-dev.com can be found here (German).
